# Dynastic Diplomacy

**Dynastic Diplomacy** expands the dynastic and succession mechanics of Victoria 3, giving monarchies new ways to pursue Personal Unions through diplomacy rather than relying solely on war goals or scripted historical content.

The mod is designed to remain lightweight and close to vanilla mechanics while making dynastic politics more meaningful and dynamic.

## Features

### Recognize Succession

Monarchies can negotiate a treaty article to recognize the ruler of another country as heir to their throne.

If the recognized ruler later succeeds to the throne, Victoria 3's existing succession mechanics will naturally establish a Personal Union between the two countries.

Existing heirs are temporarily displaced rather than permanently removed and may be restored if the succession agreement is later renounced.

### Renounce Succession

A recognized foreign ruler can formally renounce their succession rights to another country's throne.

When possible, the country's previously displaced heir will be restored to the line of succession.

### Force Abdication

Once a foreign ruler has already been recognized as heir, a treaty may demand the reigning monarch's abdication.

This immediately allows the recognized heir to succeed and may result in the establishment of a Personal Union.

### Reworked Personal Unions

Personal Unions are no longer automatically dissolved merely because one of the participating countries later ceases to be a monarchy.

Once established, the union is treated as an existing political relationship rather than a temporary monarchy-dependent arrangement.

### Dynastic AI

AI evaluation of succession treaties considers factors such as:

- Cultural affinity
- Religious affinity
- Relative economic strength
- Relative military strength
- Debt and financial distress
- Domestic turmoil
- Diplomatic attitude
- Strategic interest in the other country

Succession agreements are therefore more likely to emerge where they make political and dynastic sense.

### Personal Union Notifications

When a new Personal Union is established, players receive a notification announcing the new union of crowns.

## Design Philosophy

Dynastic Diplomacy is intended to extend Victoria 3 rather than replace its existing systems.

The mod relies wherever possible on vanilla mechanics:

- Vanilla character succession
- Vanilla Personal Union subject relationships
- Diplomatic Treaties
- Existing AI attitudes and strategic objectives

There is no separate dynasty simulation, claim-strength system, or monthly succession scanner.

The goal is to create believable dynastic politics with as little additional machinery as possible.

## Compatibility

Designed for:

**Victoria 3 1.14.x**

The mod modifies or extends:

- Treaty Articles
- Personal Union diplomatic actions
- Character roles
- Succession-related scripted logic
- Notifications

Mods that directly replace the vanilla `personal_union` diplomatic action may conflict with Dynastic Diplomacy.

## Multiplayer

Multiplayer synchronized.
